# Summary: Marketing Channel Performance
1). Objective

Evaluate channel efficiency and identify opportunities to optimize budget allocation.

## Key Findings
1. Conversion Rates Are Similar Across Channels

All major channels fall within:

12.0% – 12.8%

The difference is statistically small.

Conversion rate is NOT the differentiator.

2. Cost Per Acquisition (CPA) Is the Real Story

Lower CPA = more cost-efficient customer acquisition.

Efficient Channels:

Instagram (~11)

TikTok (~11.9)

Google Ads (~12.2)

Underperforming Channels:

Facebook (~26.5)

Email (~26.3)

Facebook and Email cost more than 2x per conversion compared to Instagram.

3. Budget Allocation Is Misaligned

Facebook Spend: ~1.68M
Instagram Spend: ~540K

Yet Instagram delivers conversions at less than half the cost.

This suggests inefficient capital deployment.

Financial Impact Scenario

If 20% of Facebook budget (~336K) is reallocated to Instagram:

At Instagram CPA (~11):

Expected additional conversions ≈ 30,500

At Facebook CPA (~26):

Same spend would generate ≈ 12,600 conversions

Potential uplift ≈ +17,900 conversions

That is a massive efficiency opportunity.

# Strategic Recommendations
1). Reduce Facebook & Email spend gradually (15–25%)
2). Reallocate toward:
Instagram
TikTok
Google Ads

3). Run controlled A/B budget experiments for 4–6 weeks
4). Investigate root cause:
Is Facebook audience too broad?
Is creative outdated?
Is targeting misaligned?

# Risk Consideration
Conversion rate similarity suggests:
Scaling Instagram too aggressively may increase CPA.

# Recommendation:
Scale gradually and monitor marginal CPA.

# Final Recommendation to Leadership

Rebalance marketing spend toward lower CPA channels to increase total conversions without increasing overall budget.
This could improve acquisition efficiency by up to 40–50%.
